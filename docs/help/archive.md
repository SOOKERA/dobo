# Archive

Put a note away without deleting it. It waits in the Archive, quiet, until you need it again.

## Archive a note

Open the note's menu (its coloured dot or **⋯**) › **Archive note**. The note leaves the board with its lines and pictures, and its reminders stay quiet while it's away. A message says "Archived "Shopping"", with **Undo** and **See archive**.

## Open the Archive

- **Archive (2)** under your notes, on the left, while some notes are archived.
- The board's right-click menu › **Archived notes…**
- The header's **⋯** menu › **Archive**.
- The **?** menu at the board's corner › **Archive**.
- **Settings › Archive › Open the archive**.

**‹ Back** returns to where you came from (Settings, say); **×** or **Esc** goes straight to the board.

## In the Archive

Each archived note shows as a card with its space, a few of its lines, what it holds ("3 to do · 2 ideas · 1 picture") and when it was archived ("Archived yesterday", "Archived on 3 November").

- **Restore**: back on the board, at its place. Its reminders ring again.
- **Delete** (the bin): deletes it. **Undo** can still bring it back.
- **Empty archive…**: asks "Delete all 5 archived notes?" first (**Keep them** / **Delete all**). Undo can bring them back until Dobo closes.

When it's empty: "Nothing in the archive".
