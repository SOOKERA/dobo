# Search

Find a note, a task or an idea in a few letters, and jump straight to it.

## Find something

1. Press **Ctrl+F**, or **/** on the board (or click **Search** in the header).
2. Type a few letters. Notes, tasks and ideas show up, with your word marked.
3. **↑** / **↓** move through them. **Enter** jumps to the note and makes it glow; on a task or an idea, the board's keyboard lands on that line (its note unfolds if it was folded), and **↑** / **↓** go on from there. **Esc** closes.

**Search in**: **All spaces** or **This space**.

Archived notes aren't searched: open the [Archive](archive.md) to see them.

## Search the settings

Inside **Settings**, **Ctrl+F** searches the settings instead. English and French words both work ("dark", "sombre", "backup", "rappel"). **Esc** clears the search, then closes Settings.

## Change the key

**Ctrl+F** can be changed in **Settings › Keyboard** (**Change**, then press the new keys). **/** always works too, except in Tidy and the Archive (where **Ctrl+F** still opens Search).
