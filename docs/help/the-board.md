# The board

Your notes sit on the board in columns, under the entry line and the Inbox strip. You arrange them by hand, like paper.

## What's on screen

From the top:

- **The header**: **All · Work · Personal**, the sun/moon (light or dark), **Style**, **New note**, **Tidy** (with how many lines wait), **Search**, **Look back on today**, **Settings**, and the window's own buttons. The **⋯** menu holds **Archive** and **Take the tour**, and in a narrow window the buttons that don't fit.
- **The entry line**, where you type, with a hint line under it (and the clock).
- **The Next line**: your next timed tasks.
- **The Inbox strip** (see [Inbox and quick dump](inbox-and-dump.md)). While you carry a task, a strip just under it opens today's hours over your notes, without moving them (see [The hours ruler](the-hours-ruler.md)).
- **Your notes**, in columns. While you carry a line, a dashed **+ New note** card waits at the end: drop the line there to start a new note with it at the end, or on any empty spot to start it right there (see [Tasks and ideas](tasks-and-ideas.md)).
- At the bottom right, **Archive (2)** while some notes are archived (its icon alone, with the count on it, in a narrow window), then **A A A** (note size) and **?** (help and the quick tour).

## Inside a note

A note shows its tasks first (a ticked one stays in its place), then its pictures, then its ideas on a lighter band, and **+ Add** at the end to write a new line there.

When every task in a note is done, the note says so: a big faint check across its lower right, its words a little softer, and **All done** under its title (its ideas still counted: "All done · 1 idea"). Tick the last task and the check draws itself in; untick one and the note is back as it was. Folded, it shows a small check before **All done**. A note with no tasks never shows it, and neither does the Inbox.

- **Rename a note**: double-click its title, or select it and press **Enter** or **F2**.
- **Fold it to its title**: the arrow on the note, or **F**. Again to unfold.
- **Open its menu**: click its coloured dot or the **⋯** on its title, or right-click the note (selected: **Shift+F10** or the Menu key).

## Move notes

- **Move a note**: drag its title bar. It lifts like paper and moves one place at a time in its column; the other notes stay where they are.
- **Make a note wide**: drag its right edge to the right and it takes two columns. Drag it back to the left for normal width. Also **Make wide** in its menu.

Columns stay put while a note grows or shrinks. A new note takes the free place at the end (made from the board's right-click menu, or from a line dropped on an empty spot: the place you chose); deleting a note lets the board settle again.

## A note's menu

- **Colour**: the **Soft** or **Vivid** palette, ten colours each, plus **Custom**. Right-click a colour (or **Edit**) to change it and give it a name.
- **Space**: **Work** or **Personal**.
- **Paper**: **Plain**, **Ruled**, **Grid** or **Antique**.
- **Write into this note**: the entry line shows "Into Shopping" and writes there until you click its **×** (**Back to the Inbox**).
- **Make wide** / **Make normal width**, **Fold to its title** / **Unfold**.
- **Move done tasks to the end** / **Keep done tasks in place**: where its done tasks show. At first they stay where they are, so a list of steps keeps its order. Moved to the end, they gather after the open ones: a task you tick shows its tick where it is for a moment, then glides there. Their own order never changes, so you can switch back at any time. (The Inbox keeps its done tasks in place.)
- **Save as a template**: start new notes from it later.
- **Save as a picture**: a PNG in **Pictures › Dobo**, also copied so you can paste it into a message.
- **Copy as text**: its title, its tasks as a checklist and its ideas (a line's link or file under it), to paste anywhere. A short "Copied" says it's done.
- **Archive note**: takes it off the board, to bring it back later (see [Archive](archive.md)).
- **Delete note…**: asks "Delete "Shopping" and its lines?" first. **Undo** can still bring it back.

## A new note

- **Right-click an empty spot of the board › New note**: an empty note appears right where you clicked, then glides up its column to sit under the note above (the board never leaves a gap; clicked in an empty column with an empty one on its left, it glides over to the first empty column), its title ready to type. Type a title and press **Enter** to go on to its first line. **Esc** with nothing typed, or a click elsewhere, takes it away again. In **All**, it goes to the space of the notes around it (else Work).
- **Right-click an empty spot › New note from a template…**, or click **New note** in the header: give it a **Title**, pick **Blank note**, one of Dobo's own templates or one of yours (you see a **Preview**), and click **Create note**. From the right-click menu, the note lands where you clicked.
- **Dobo's own templates** come first: **Today** (a few blank tasks), **Ideas** (a few blank ideas) and **Plan** (a few blank tasks, then blank ideas). Their blank lines show what goes where: click one and type, and **Enter** moves on to the next. They're not lines yet, so they never count as "to do"; **Backspace** in an empty one takes it away, and those left go when Dobo closes.
- **Make your own**: **Save as a template** in any note's **⋯** menu. Your templates follow Dobo's own in the New note window.
- Or type a line with **#** and a new name: "Book the tickets #holidays" makes the note "holidays" when none matches.

## Work and Personal

Each note belongs to a space, **Work** or **Personal**, and each space has its own Inbox. **All · Work · Personal** in the header chooses what the board shows. New lines go to the space on screen (to Work while **All** shows).

To switch by the clock, turn on **Settings › Work hours › Switch between Work and Personal by the clock**, and choose the days and hours (09:00 to 19:00 at first). Hours can cross midnight, such as 22:00 to 06:00: they end the next morning and count for the day they start, so Saturday 02:00 is still Friday's night. Reminders from the other space still ring: the board switches to it for that reminder and offers to go back. You can still switch by hand.

## Note size

**Small**, **Normal** or **Large**: the **A A A** at the board's corner, **Ctrl+wheel**, **Ctrl +** / **Ctrl −** / **Ctrl 0** (back to Normal), or **Settings › Notes › Note size**.

## Undo

Nearly everything can be undone, a new note too: **Ctrl+Z**, then **Ctrl+Y** (or **Ctrl+Shift+Z**) to redo, or the **Undo** button in the short message at the bottom. One step at a time. Inside a text field, Ctrl+Z undoes your typing instead.
