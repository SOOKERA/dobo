# The hours ruler

Pick up a task and drop it on the clock. Your notes stay where they are: the hours of your day wait just under the Inbox strip, and open only when you bring the task to them.

## Drag a task onto the hours

1. Press on a task's words and start dragging it. Nothing on the board moves: a quiet dashed strip, **Drop here for a time**, shows just under the Inbox strip, over the top of your notes.
2. Carry the task onto that strip (or just next to it). Today's hours open in its place, floating over the notes below, from now on, with "now" marked and your other timed tasks as dots. The board dims a little meanwhile.
3. Move along the hours. Under the slip, the time it would get: 14:00, 14:15, 14:30…
4. Drop it on an hour. The task shows its new time, and the hours go away.

- It snaps to the quarter hour: "snaps to 15 min · Shift for exact". Hold **Shift** for the exact minute.
- Drop it on **Tomorrow**, at the end of the hours, for the start of tomorrow's hours, or hold over it to see them. **Back to today** returns.
- Changed your mind? Carry the task on down onto the board: after a short moment the hours fold back into the strip, and you can drop the task on a note as usual. Bring it back to the strip and they open again.
- **Esc** while carrying sends the task back where it was.
- A task in the Inbox is right above the strip: drop it on an hour there, then drag it on into its note.
- With the Inbox unfolded, the strip is under its lines. With the board scrolled so the Inbox is out of sight, the strip stays at the top of the board, always within reach.

The hours shown run from now to the end of your day (**Settings › My day**, 08:00 to 22:00 at first). A task in **Work**, with **Settings › Work hours** on, goes to a little after your work hours end (the next morning, for hours that cross midnight), and its **Tomorrow** is the next work day (by its name when that isn't tomorrow). The hours read "14", or "2 PM" when Windows uses a 12-hour clock. Late in the evening, with less than half an hour left, the ruler shows tomorrow's hours at once.

## Without dragging

Open the task's menu (**⋯**) › **Pick on today's hours**: the same hours open in the same place, just under the Inbox strip. Click an hour. **Close the hours** leaves it as it was.

From the keyboard, the hours take the keys as they open: **←** / **→** move by 15 minutes (with **Shift**, by an hour), **Home** / **End** go to the first and last hour, **Enter** sets the time. **Tab** goes on to **Tomorrow** (**Enter** picks it) and **Esc** closes.

## The Next line

The **Next** line, under the entry line, shows your next timed tasks, so what's coming is always in sight. It stays as it is while you carry a task.
