# Docked or floating

Dobo sits at the edge of your screen and slides in with one key, or stays out as a floating window, like a sticky note that's always in sight. Choose in **Settings › Window**.

## Docked to the edge

- **Ctrl+Shift+Space** slides it in on the screen your mouse is on, and again slides it away. A click elsewhere hides it too, and so does **Esc** once nothing is open.
- **Side of the screen**: **Left** or **Right**.
- **Width**: **Narrow**, **Medium** or **Wide**, or drag the panel's inner edge to any width (it shows **Custom · 640 px**). Dobo remembers it for each screen.
- **Keep the panel open**: the pin in the header. When it's on, the button is filled; the panel stays out when you click elsewhere.
- **Full screen**: when the panel is kept open and another app goes full screen on the same screen (a video, a game, a presentation), it steps aside and comes back afterwards.

### Open it with the mouse

**Also open when the mouse reaches the right edge** (or left): push the pointer against the edge for a moment. With **Peek at the titles first**, your notes' titles slide out like index cards: click one to open the panel on that note, or stay at the edge to open everything.

## A floating window

- Put it anywhere, any size, on any screen; Dobo remembers where it was.
- **Move it** by its top: the header on the board, or the top of whatever is open (Settings, Style, the Archive, Tidy, the picture viewer). Drag it against the screen's edge to snap it, as with any window.
- **Always on top**: its button in the header (in the **⋯** menu when the window is narrow), or **Settings › Window › Always on top**. When it's on, the button is filled.
- **Minimise**, and **×** to close it to the tray (Dobo keeps running).

## Several screens

**Settings › Screen › Show on**: **Screen with the mouse**, or **One screen** (click the screen you want; Dobo uses it while it's connected).

## A narrow window

In a narrow window, the header goes onto two rows, then tucks the buttons that don't fit (**Look back on today**, **Settings**, **Always on top**…) into its **⋯** menu.
