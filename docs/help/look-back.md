# Look back

A short look at your day: what got done, what's still open, and today's new ideas. No scores.

## In the evening

Once a day, Dobo offers a look back at the top of the board. It comes at the end of your day (**Settings › My day**, **ends at** 22:00 at first), or at the end of your work hours on work days if **Settings › Work hours › Switch between Work and Personal by the clock** is on. Work hours that cross midnight (22:00 to 06:00) bring it the next morning, when they end: Monday's night brings "That was Monday." on Tuesday at 06:00, and it stays until that day's own. It counts the whole night, up to 06:00; Tuesday's then starts there.

A Windows notification tells you it's there: "That was Tuesday." On a day when the look back would be empty (nothing ticked, nothing still open, no new idea), there's no notification. The look back itself says "That was Tuesday." and shows:

- **Done today**: what you ticked ("Nothing ticked. Some days are like that." on a quiet day). A repeating task counts on each day you ticked it, even after it has come back for its next time.
- **Still open from today**: each task due today (or yesterday) and not ticked, with **Tomorrow** (the next day, at the same time), **Pick a day** (type "Monday 9am", "Friday"…) or **No time**.
- **New ideas**: the ideas you wrote today.

It stays until the next morning (when your day starts): after midnight it is still the evening's, and its **Tomorrow** is the day that has just begun. **Close** puts it away until tomorrow evening: "See you tomorrow." Clicked later, its notification opens the look back of the day it was about.

## Any time

Click **Look back on today** (the sunset button) in the header. Before your day (or your work hours) is over, it says "Today so far." and doesn't say goodbye yet. At night, in work hours that cross midnight (22:00 to 06:00), it's still the day the night began, with all that happened since 22:00: "Tonight so far." The look back opens at the top of the board, at its full size, and the board moves down below it; scroll the page for the rest. The button stays filled while it's open: click it again (**Close the look back**), or press **Esc**, to close it.

Closing one you opened during the day keeps the evening's look back for later. Once the evening's look back is closed (after midnight, or the morning after a night's work), the button shows the day that has begun, so far.
