# Look back

A short look at your day: what got done, what's still open, and today's new ideas. No scores.

## In the evening

Once a day, Dobo offers a look back at the top of the board. It comes at the end of your day (**Settings › My day**, **ends at** 22:00 at first), or at the end of your work hours on work days if **Settings › Work hours › Switch between Work and Personal by the clock** is on.

A Windows notification tells you it's there: "That was Tuesday." On a day when nothing happened (nothing ticked, due or written), there's no notification. The look back itself says "That was Tuesday." and shows:

- **Done today**: what you ticked ("Nothing ticked. Some days are like that." on a quiet day).
- **Still open from today**: for each open task, **Tomorrow**, **Pick a day** (type "lundi 9h", "friday"…) or **No time**.
- **New ideas**: the ideas you wrote today.

**Close** (or **Esc**) puts it away until tomorrow evening: "See you tomorrow."

## Any time

Click **Look back on today** (the sunset button) in the header. The look back opens at the top of the board, at its full size, and the board moves down below it; scroll the page for the rest. The button stays filled while it's open: click it again (**Close the look back**) to close it.

Closing one you opened during the day keeps the evening's look back for later.
