# What's new

## 1.0.0

The first release of Dobo: tasks and ideas on a board of notes, for Windows, in English and French.

### Capture

- Type a line and press **Enter** for a task or **Shift+Enter** for an idea. Write the time in plain words ("tomorrow 9am", "demain 15h"), and end with **!** for a deadline.
- **Ctrl+Shift+D** opens the quick dump bar from any app. It can keep a link to the web page you were reading.
- **+ Add** at the end of every note, and a list of notes as soon as you type **#**.

### The board

- The **Inbox** is a strip across the top of the board: unfold it, pin it open, and drag its lines onto your notes, or let **Tidy** sort them one card at a time.
- **Steps** under a task, with a ring round its checkbox that fills as you tick them.
- An **Archive** for the notes you put away, to restore or delete them later.

### Times

- Reminders and deadlines you answer from the Windows notification: **Done**, **10 min**, **Later…**, and **I'm on it** for a deadline.
- The **hours ruler**: drag a task onto today's or tomorrow's hours.
- **Look back** on the day, once in the evening, or any time from the header.

### Style and Windows

- Nine styles of ours, watercolour paintings, your own picture, and themes that change with dark mode.
- Docked at the edge of the screen or as a floating window, always one shortcut away (**Ctrl+Shift+Space**).
- **Dobo-Setup.exe** installs it for you, without admin rights; **Dobo.exe** is the portable version. Both update themselves once a day (**Settings › Updates**): each update's signature is checked first, it's used the next time Dobo starts, and if it doesn't start properly the version you had is put back. Your notes are never touched.
