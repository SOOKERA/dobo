# What's new

## 1.2.2

Dobo 1.2.2 makes your own background picture easier to find, shows more clearly what each background offers, and keeps Dobo to its own folders.

### Highlights

- **Import a picture.** The last background tile now says **Import a picture**, with an import icon: click it to choose a picture of your own. Once you have, the tile shows your picture and reads **Your picture**, and Dobo remembers it, also after a restart. See [Style and themes](style-and-themes.md).
- **A background’s own choices, together.** What the chosen background offers now sits in a soft card right under the tiles, named after it: **Paper · colours**, **Watercolour · mixes**, **Your picture**. Pick another background and the card changes with it. See [Style and themes](style-and-themes.md).
- **Dobo stays in its own folders.** Your pictures, link previews and backgrounds are only ever read from Dobo’s own data folder. A picture from anywhere else (for example in a backup from another computer) shows as a quiet placeholder and is never fetched, so Dobo never reaches another computer on its own.

## 1.2.1

Dobo 1.2.1 keeps a repeating task on one line with its history, leaves done tasks where they are, and makes the hours easier to read.

### Highlights

- **A repeating task is one line now.** Tick it and it stays ticked, showing its next time, until its next day comes; then it opens again by itself, in the same place, at the start of your day (or by its own time, if that comes first). A daily task comes back tomorrow, a weekly one on its next day, a monthly one next month. Its steps start fresh each time. Ticked copies left from before stay until you delete them. See [Times and reminders](times-and-reminders.md).
- **History, for every repeating task.** Its menu (**⋯**, or right-click) › **History…** shows how it went: a month of days for a daily task, a year of weeks for a weekly one, a year of months for a monthly one. Done, missed and still to come at a glance, and a short line in its own rhythm: "Done 7 of the last 8 weeks · 3 weeks in a row". See [Times and reminders](times-and-reminders.md).
- **Done tasks stay where they are.** A ticked task keeps its place, so a list of steps keeps its order. To gather a note’s done tasks at the end, as before, choose **Move done tasks to the end** in its **⋯** menu. See [The board](the-board.md).
- **Clearer hours.** When you carry a task onto the hours, both ends are named: the time now, under its line, and when your day ends. "Now" has a sun by day and a moon in the evening, and a short evening names its half hours too. See [The hours ruler](the-hours-ruler.md).

### Also

- **Archive (2)**, shown while some notes are archived, has moved to the board’s bottom right corner, beside the note sizes and **?**. See [Archive](archive.md).

## 1.2.0

Dobo 1.2.0 keeps your notes in place while you carry a task, starts a new note wherever you want one, shows at a glance when a note is all done, and tells you what’s new after each update.

### Highlights

- **Your notes stay put when you pick up a task.** The hours used to open at once and push every note down. Now a quiet strip, **Drop here for a time**, floats just under the Inbox: carry the task onto it and today’s hours open right there, over the notes; move on down to a note and they fold away. A task in the Inbox gets its time in one move, before you file it, and **Pick on today’s hours** opens the same hours in the same place. See [The hours ruler](the-hours-ruler.md).
- **Drag a line out to start a new note.** While you carry a line, from the Inbox or any note, a dashed **+ New note** card waits at the end of the board. Drop the line there, or on any empty spot to start it right there: a new note grows in, named after the line, with the line inside as its first line (its time, steps, link and pictures too). A line’s menu has it too: **Make a new note of it**. One **Ctrl+Z** puts everything back. See [Tasks and ideas](tasks-and-ideas.md).
- **A new note right where you want it.** Right-click an empty spot of the board › **New note**: it appears there and settles under the note above, its title ready to type (**Esc** with nothing typed takes it away again). **New note from a template…** lands its note there too, and Dobo now has three templates of its own, **Today**, **Ideas** and **Plan**, with blank lines that show what goes where. Make your own with **Save as a template** in any note’s **⋯** menu. See [The board](the-board.md).
- **A note whose tasks are all done says so.** Tick its last task and a big faint check draws itself across the note, its words soften a little, and the line under its title reads **All done** (ideas still counted: "All done · 1 idea"). Easy to spot from across the board; untick a task and the note is back as it was. See [The board](the-board.md).
- **What’s new, after each update.** The first time Dobo opens after an update, it shows what the new version brings (and the versions in between, if you skipped a few), once, then gets out of the way. A new install shows nothing. Read it again any time: **What’s new** in the **?** menu at the board’s corner, or in **Settings › Updates**. See [Updates](install-and-update.md#updates).

### Also

- **Double-clicking a note’s buttons** (its colour dot, **⋯** or the fold arrow) no longer starts renaming the note: the button just does its own thing twice.
- **Updates come second in Settings,** right after **Appearance**: one line says which Dobo you have and where its update stands, with its button beside it, then the switch for the daily check and **What’s new**. See [Updates](install-and-update.md#updates).

## 1.1.1

Dobo 1.1.1 lets you tell Sookera when something goes wrong, in a few words, and see first everything that goes with them.

### Highlights

- **Report a problem,** in the **?** menu at the board’s corner and in **Settings › Help**. Say what happened, see everything that goes with your words first, then **Send report**, **Send by email** or **Copy details**. Dobo’s own problem notes can go with it, with your user folder, file names and anything in quotes taken out, and you can leave them out. Nothing is ever sent by itself. See [Report a problem](report-a-problem.md).
- **Ideas and questions** have a home: **Share it on GitHub Discussions**, at the bottom of the same page.

### Also

- **Setup’s switches slide** between off and on, in Setup and in the uninstaller (at once when Windows’ animation effects are off).

## 1.1.0

Dobo 1.1.0 speaks French from Setup on, takes even better care of your notes, and fits in more closely with Windows.

### Highlights

- **Setup in French.** Setup now has **English | Français** at the top (English first), and Dobo opens in the language you pick.
- **If your notes don’t open,** Dobo shows a calm recovery screen instead of an error: **Use the repaired notes**, or put back the newest backup. The damaged file is always kept aside, never deleted.
- **Right-click menus of Dobo’s own** on notes and lines (or **Shift+F10** and the Menu key), with **Copy as text** to paste a whole note anywhere.
- **Grouped reminders.** When several ring at once, or came due while the PC slept, one notification holds them all: “3 reminders”, with **Open Dobo**.
- **Your Windows clock.** Times show as “15:00” or “3:00 PM”, the way Windows does, and follow it the moment you change it.
- **Work hours across midnight.** A night shift from 22:00 to 06:00 switches between Work and Personal at the right times, and its look back covers the whole night.
- **Lighter pictures.** The board shows smaller copies of your pictures, so it opens faster and uses far less memory. The viewer still shows the original, and GIFs keep moving.
- **Files on network shares.** A file in a shared folder or on a NAS asks once for that computer (**Allow**), then opens like any other.
- **Windows Sticky Notes asks first.** Nothing comes in by itself any more: “Bring in your 5 Windows Sticky Notes?”, with **Import** or **Not now**.
- **The true logo,** the textured note, in the header, Setup, the taskbar and Start.
- **A soft crossfade** when you change the style or the theme, light and dark included.
- **The terms in French,** a courtesy translation with the English original one click away.

### Also

- **Backups** come in kinds: daily, made by hand, before a restore, and a new one before each update. Each kind is kept apart, so the ones you make never push out the daily ones. And Dobo starts faster.
- **Steps** stay quiet while their task is ticked.
- **Repeats** keep their time when the clocks change.
- **Keyboard and screen readers:** the arrows move between notes as they sit on screen, the hours ruler works as a slider, and buttons say which note or line they act on.
- **Many fixes,** among them “every Mon and Thu” ringing on Thursdays too, “tonight” in the evening and “at 3” in the afternoon.

### Going back to 1.0.0

If you put Dobo 1.0.0 back, it rings the steps of ticked tasks again, and keeps only 14 backups in all (it deletes the oldest ones beyond that).

## 1.0.0

The first release of Dobo: tasks and ideas on a board of notes, for Windows, in English and French.

### Capture

- Type a line and press **Enter** for a task or **Shift+Enter** for an idea. Write the time in plain words ("tomorrow 9am", "demain 15h"), and end with **!** for a deadline.
- **Ctrl+Shift+D** opens the quick dump bar from any app. It can keep a link to the web page you were reading.
- **+ Add** at the end of every note, and a list of notes as soon as you type **#**.

### The board

- The **Inbox** is a strip across the top of the board: unfold it, pin it open, and drag its lines onto your notes, or let **Tidy** sort them one card at a time.
- **Steps** under a task, with a ring round its checkbox that fills as you tick them.
- An **Archive** for the notes you put away, to restore or delete them later.

### Times

- Reminders and deadlines you answer from the Windows notification: **Done**, **10 min**, **Later…**, and **I’m on it** for a deadline.
- The **hours ruler**: drag a task onto today’s or tomorrow’s hours.
- **Look back** on the day, once in the evening, or any time from the header.

### Style and Windows

- Nine styles of ours, watercolour paintings, your own picture, and themes that change with dark mode.
- Docked at the edge of the screen or as a floating window, always one shortcut away (**Ctrl+Shift+Space**).
- **Dobo-Setup.exe** installs it for you, without admin rights; **Dobo.exe** is the portable version. Both update themselves once a day (**Settings › Updates**): each update’s signature is checked first, it’s used the next time Dobo starts, and if it doesn’t start properly the version you had is put back. Your notes are never touched.
