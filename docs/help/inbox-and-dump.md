# Inbox and quick dump

Write it now, sort it later. Everything without a place goes to the Inbox, and you put it away when you have a minute.

## The Inbox strip

The Inbox is a strip across the full width of the board, at the top. Folded, it says how many lines wait: **Inbox · 3 waiting**, with **Tidy** and its pin. When nothing waits, it says **Inbox · empty** with the hint "Type above, or Ctrl+Shift+D from anywhere", and **Tidy** steps away until something comes in.

- **Open it**: click the strip, or select it and press **Enter**. It unfolds downwards and your notes glide down to make room. Its lines show in two columns when there's room.
- **Fold it**: click it again, or press **Esc**.
- **Keep it open**: click its pin (**Keep the Inbox open**). The pin fills when it's on, and Dobo remembers it. Clicking a pinned strip folds it and lets go of the pin.
- **Put a line away**: drag it out of the strip and drop it on the note it belongs to. The notes you can drop on light up. You can also drop it on a task (it becomes a step) or on the hours (it gets a time).
- **Bring a line back**: drag any line from a note onto the strip.

The Inbox can't be moved, renamed, archived or deleted. Each space has its own: with **All**, both show, Work first, each under its name.

When it's empty, it says: "Nothing waiting. Lines typed without a #note land here, and so do quick dumps (Ctrl+Shift+D)."

## The quick dump bar

Press **Ctrl+Shift+D** from any app (or **+ Dump** in the tray menu). A small bar appears in the middle of the screen. Type, press **Enter**, and it's in the Inbox: "Dumped. 3 waiting in the Inbox." Nothing to decide, and you're back to what you were doing.

- **Esc**, or a click elsewhere, closes it and keeps what you typed for next time.
- **Paste a picture** into it (**Ctrl+V**): it comes with the line.
- The bar keeps your words as they are: it doesn't read a time or a **#note**. Give the line a time later, in **Tidy** (**When?**) or on the board.
- It goes to the Personal Inbox while the board shows **Personal**, otherwise to the Work one.

### The page you were reading

If a web browser was in front, the bar shows **Keeps the link to**, then the page's title and its site. The line then carries a small card with the page's title and picture; click it to open the page in your browser. Click **Leave it out** if you don't want the link (**Keep it** brings it back).

To show that card, Dobo fetches the page's title and picture once (see [Privacy](privacy.md)).

## Tidy

**Tidy** (on the strip, or in the header, with how many lines wait) goes through the Inbox one card at a time:

1. **It's**: **A task** or **An idea** (**T** / **I**).
2. **When?**: an optional time, like "tomorrow 10am" or "Friday 3pm". End it with **!** for a deadline.
3. **Into which note?**: when the words match, one is suggested ("Suggested: Shopping, it mentions milk"). **1–9** picks a note, or type a **New note's name**.
4. **Where in Shopping?**: **A new task in the note**, **A new idea in the note**, or **A step of the task** (**↑** / **↓**).
5. **Put it in Shopping** (**Enter**). **Keep for later** (**L**) skips the card for now; **D** deletes it.

"Landed in Shopping" shows where it went, and the next card rises. At the end: "Inbox clear. Everything has a place. Nothing more to tidy." **Back to the board** closes it.

### A nudge to tidy

Dobo reminds you to tidy once a day (at 17:30 at first) and when many things wait (10 at first). Change both, or turn them off, in **Settings › Reminders › Tidy the Inbox**.
