# Times and reminders

Write a time the way you'd say it, and Dobo reminds you. End a line with ! and it becomes a deadline that starts early and insists a little more as it gets close.

## A time in plain words

Write it in the line, in English or French (Dobo understands both, whatever its own language): "Call Anna tomorrow 15h", "dentist friday 9am", "rapport lundi 14h30", "3pm", "demain 10h".

- The line under the field shows how Dobo read it before you press **Enter**: "Reminds you at the time · end with ! for a deadline".
- The time is taken out of your words and shown as a small pill on the task.
- A day without an hour ("tomorrow") means 09:00.
- Ideas don't take a time. Make it a task first if you want one.

## A deadline: end with !

"Send the report friday 17h!" is a deadline. The hint says "Deadline: rings early, then more often", and its pill has a small hourglass.

A deadline follows a **rhythm**. **Normal**, the first one, starts 60 minutes before, rings again at 30, 15 and 5 minutes before, then every 5 minutes after the time, 6 times at most. **Gentle** is calmer, **Insistent** stronger, and **Custom** is yours.

- Change the default in **Settings › Reminders › Default rhythm for deadlines**.
- Change one task: its menu › **Kind of reminder**: **Rings at the time** or **The rhythm**, and the rhythm's minutes.

A plain reminder rings at the time, then every 5 minutes until you answer, 6 times at most.

## Set or change a time later

- **The task's menu** (**⋯**): type a **New time** ("demain 10h", "friday 15h") and click **Set**, or **Remove the time**.
- **The hours ruler**: drag the task onto an hour, or **Pick on today's hours** in its menu (see [The hours ruler](the-hours-ruler.md)).
- **Tidy**: the **When?** field.
- **While editing**: see "Dates written in a note" in [Tasks and ideas](tasks-and-ideas.md).

## Repeats

Write it in the line: "water the plants every Monday", "stand-up every weekday 9h30", "pay rent every 1st", "every 2 weeks", "tous les jours 8h". The first one is set for the next matching day, and the pill shows a repeat mark.

Or use the task's menu › **Repeat**: **Never**, **Day**, **Weekday**, **Week** (pick the days) or **Month** (a **Day of the month**, or the **Last day**); **Every how many**; and when it **Ends**: **Never**, **After** a number of times, or **On a date**. Ticking a repeating task makes the next one.

## Answer a reminder

A reminder comes as a Windows notification, and shows on the **Now card** at the top of Dobo. Answer either one:

- **Done** ticks the task.
- **10 min** (or **1 hour**, on a plain reminder) rings again later.
- **Later…** opens Dobo with more choices: **1 hour**, **This evening**, **Tomorrow morning**, **Next Monday**, or **Pick a time** ("demain 14h", "friday 9am").
- **I'm on it** (a deadline): Dobo stops ringing, and a little later asks "Is "Send the report" really done?" with **Yes, done** and **Not yet**.

When several ring at once, the Now card shows **2 more**: click it to see the next one.

"This evening" and "Tomorrow morning" follow **Settings › My day** (08:00 to 22:00 at first).

## Reminders only ring while Dobo runs

Closing the window keeps Dobo in the tray, so reminders still ring. **Quit Dobo** stops them. Keep **Settings › Start › Start Dobo with the computer** on so it's always there, and let Windows show Dobo's notifications.

A reminder from the other space (Work or Personal) still rings: the board switches to it for that reminder, with a button to go back. An archived note's reminders stay quiet until you restore it.
