# Style and themes

Paper, cork, kraft, felt, terrazzo, watercolour, blueprint or a picture of your own: make the board feel like yours. Changes show live, and Undo works.

## The Style panel

Click **Style** in the header (**Ctrl+Shift+S**), or right-click an empty spot of the board › **Change background…**. The panel opens beside the board. Click outside it (or press **Ctrl+Shift+S** again, or **Esc**) to close it.

- **Our styles**: **Plain**, **Paper day**, **Dotted journal**, **Cork board**, **Kraft workshop**, **Terrazzo**, **Watercolour**, **Blueprint**, **Felt night**.
- **Background**: pick a texture, then one of its colours, or any colour you like. **Your picture** uses a picture of yours, softened and dimmed so your notes stay easy to read.
- **Notes held by**: **Auto** (what suits the background), **Pins**, **Tape** or **None**.
- **Note size**, and **New notes start in**: one colour, or **Turns** (each new note takes the next colour).

## Your own styles

Change anything and the panel says "Changed from Cork board". Give it a name and click **Save as my style**: it joins **Your styles**. **Reset** goes back to where you started. On one of yours, **Update "My desk"** keeps the change in it. Its small menu renames, duplicates or deletes it (**F2** or a double-click renames too).

## Watercolour

Pick **Watercolour** to see our five paintings. **Repaint** makes a fresh painting in the same colours.

- **More mixes**: more colour mixes, grouped by mood.
- **Customise**: choose the pattern (**Scattered**, **Sweep**, **Corners**, **One patch**), 1 to 3 colours from the watercolour paints or your own, and white or dark paper. **Save as my mix** keeps it under **Watercolour · yours**.

## Light and dark

- **Settings › Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark**, **Follow Windows** or **By hours** (dark between two times, 19:00 to 07:00 at first). The Style panel has the same choice.
- The sun/moon button in the header switches right away.
- Dark mode darkens the app and the notes. The board keeps its own look, unless your theme has a dark style.

## Themes

A theme is a **Light style** and a **Dark style** kept together: when dark mode turns on, the board changes to the dark one. **Paper** is the first one: Paper day by day, a dark paper at night.

In the Style panel, open **Themes**. Use one of **Our themes**, click a half of a card to change that style, or make a **New theme** and **Save as my theme**. **Your themes** have a **⋯** menu to edit, rename, duplicate, export or delete them.

## Share a look, or take it to another PC

**Style › Export & import**: save **This theme**, **All my themes** or **Everything** (themes, styles, watercolour mixes, your colours, dark-mode hours) as a small `.ztheme` file.

To add one, choose the file or drop it on the board, see what's inside, then click **Add to my themes**. Nothing you have is replaced: a name that's already taken gets "(2)". A look file never contains your notes.
