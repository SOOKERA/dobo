# Steps

Break a bigger task into steps, tick them one by one, and see how far along it is at a glance.

## Add steps

- Open the task's menu (**⋯**) › **Add a step**, type the step, press **Enter**. The field stays open for the next one ("A step, then Enter").
- Or drag a line onto the task. While it's over the task, the task says **Drop to make it a step**. Drop it there.
- In **Tidy**, choose **A step of the task** under **Where in…?**

An idea can have steps too: drop a line on it and it becomes its first step (**Drop to make it the first step**).

## See how far along it is

- A thin ring round the task's checkbox fills as steps are ticked, going round like a clock hand, and closes when they're all done.
- The task also shows the count, like **2/5**. Point at it for "2 of 5 steps done".
- An idea has no checkbox: it shows a small pie beside its count instead.

Ticking the task itself puts its ring away.

## Change steps

Steps are lines like any other: tick them, edit them with a double-click, delete them, or drag them out to make them lines of their own. **Undo** brings back what you change.
