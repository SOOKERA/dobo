# Getting started

Dobo keeps your tasks and ideas on a board of notes, one shortcut away. This is your first few minutes with it.

## First launch

The first time, Dobo opens a small welcome window in the middle of your screen. At its top you can pick the language: **Follow Windows**, **English** or **Français**.

1. **How should it sit on your screen?**
   - **Docked to the edge**: it slides in with a shortcut, on the screen your mouse is on, and hides when you click away.
   - **A floating window**: put it anywhere, any size, on any screen, and keep it above other windows if you like.

   Click **Continue**. You can change this any time in **Settings › Window** (see [Docked or floating](docked-and-floating.md)).
2. **A few keys to remember**: **Ctrl+Shift+Space** opens or hides Dobo and **Ctrl+Shift+D** is the quick dump into the Inbox: these two work from anywhere. **Ctrl+F** searches, inside Dobo. Change them in **Settings › Keyboard**.
3. **Start Dobo with Windows** is on. Reminders only ring while Dobo runs, and it waits quietly in the tray.
4. **Windows Sticky Notes**: if you use them, the welcome asks "Bring in your 4 Windows Sticky Notes?". **Import** copies them onto the board (a note each; a one-line one goes into the Inbox). **Not now** leaves them, and Dobo doesn't ask again. Nothing is brought in without asking. Dobo reads a copy of Sticky Notes' database on this PC, so your Sticky Notes stay as they are and nothing leaves your PC. Later, it's in **Settings › Start › Bring in the notes from Windows Sticky Notes**. With no sticky notes on this PC, this part isn't shown.

Click **Start**. The welcome window closes and Dobo goes to its place.

If some of your sticky notes mention a time ("demain 9h", "Monday 14h"), a card **Times found in your sticky notes** offers **Add these reminders**. Their words stay as you wrote them. **No thanks** keeps them as plain lines.

## The quick tour

A moment after the first launch, Dobo offers a tour, once: "New here? A one-minute tour." with **Take the tour**. It never starts by itself.

Eight short steps, under a minute:

1. **Dump it, from anywhere**: the quick dump bar.
2. **It waits in the Inbox**: the strip at the top of the board.
3. **Sort it into notes**: drag a line onto its note, or let **Tidy** do it.
4. **Give it a time**: write it as you type, and **!** for a deadline.
5. **Or drag it onto the hours**: the hours ruler.
6. **Or make it a step**: drop a line onto a bigger task.
7. **Make it yours**: the **Style** button.
8. **Help is always here**: the **?** at the board's corner.

**→** or **Enter** goes on, **←** goes back, **Esc** or **×** closes, and **Skip** ends it. Nothing in the tour is kept: its sample line, "Call the bank", never rings and leaves nothing behind.

Take it again whenever you like: **Take the tour** in the **?** menu (or press **F1**), in the header's **⋯** menu, in the board's right-click menu, or in **Settings › Help**.

## Open and hide Dobo

- Press **Ctrl+Shift+Space** from any app, or click Dobo's icon in the tray.
- **Docked**: Dobo hides when you click elsewhere, or with **Esc**. The pin in the header (**Keep the panel open**) keeps it out.
- **Floating**: **×** closes the window to the tray. Dobo keeps running, so reminders still ring.
- Right-click the tray icon for **Open Dobo**, **+ Dump** and **Quit Dobo**. **Quit Dobo** stops it completely, and reminders with it.

## Your first lines

Click the entry line at the top (it's ready as soon as Dobo opens), type, and press **Enter** for a task or **Shift+Enter** for an idea. Without a **#note**, the line lands in the Inbox. See [Tasks and ideas](tasks-and-ideas.md) and [Inbox and quick dump](inbox-and-dump.md).

## Help inside Dobo

- The **?** at the board's bottom-right corner opens a small menu: **Take the tour**, **Keyboard shortcuts**, **Archive** and **Help & settings**. **F1** opens it; **F1** again opens **Settings › Help**.
- **Settings › Help** has the tour, **Keys to know** and **Gestures**.
- Point at any button: its tooltip names it and shows its keys.
