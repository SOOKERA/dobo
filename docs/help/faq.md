# Questions

Short answers to the things people ask most.

**Where are my notes kept?**
On your PC only, in the data folder shown in **Settings › About** (**Open folder** opens it). There's no account. See [Backups and your data](backups-and-data.md).

**Can I back up and restore?**
Yes. Dobo backs up your notes and pictures once a day and keeps the last 14 (**Settings › Backups**). You can choose the folder, click **Back up now**, and **Restore…** any backup in the list. Before a restore, your current notes are backed up too.

**Can I move my notes to another PC?**
Yes: copy a backup to the new PC, choose its folder in **Settings › Backups**, and restore it. A portable Dobo moves with its folder. See [Move your notes to another PC](backups-and-data.md#move-your-notes-to-another-pc).

**Why didn't a reminder ring?**
Reminders only ring while Dobo runs. Closing the window keeps it in the tray; **Quit Dobo** stops it. Keep **Settings › Start › Start Dobo with Windows** on, and check that Windows lets Dobo show notifications (and that Do not disturb is off). A note in the Archive stays quiet until you restore it.

**Where did my line go?**
A line without a **#note** goes to the Inbox, the strip at the top of the board. Click the strip to open it.

**How do I add something to a note I already have?**
Click **+ Add** at the end of the note (or its empty space), or type the line with **#** and the note's name in the entry line.

**I archived a note. How do I get it back?**
Open the Archive (**Archive (2)** at the bottom of the board, or the **?** menu) and click **Restore**. See [Archive](archive.md).

**How do I see the tour again?**
**Take the tour** in the **?** menu at the board's corner (**F1**), the header's **⋯** menu, the board's right-click menu, or **Settings › Help**.

**Will it change my Windows Sticky Notes?**
No. Dobo reads a copy of them, and brings them in only when you ask: on the first start, or later from **Settings › Start**.

**How do I switch to French (or English)?**
**Settings › Appearance › Language / Langue**: **Follow Windows**, **English** or **Français**. It changes at once, no restart. Dobo understands times in both languages, whichever one it's in.

**How do I turn on dark mode?**
**Settings › Appearance** (**Light**, **Dark**, **Follow Windows**, **By hours**), the Style panel, or the sun/moon button in the header.

**A shortcut doesn't work.**
Another app may already use it. Choose new keys in **Settings › Keyboard** (**Change**, then press the keys). Dobo says so when the keys you pick are taken.

**The floating window won't move.**
Drag it by its top: the header, or the top row of whatever is open. Buttons and fields there don't move it.

**Does Dobo need the internet?**
No. It goes online only for link cards of the pages you dump, and once a day to check for updates (unless you turn that off in **Settings › Updates**), and never before you accept its terms of use. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

**How do I update Dobo?**
You don't have to: once a day, Dobo downloads and checks a new version by itself, and uses it the next time it starts (or now, with **Restart now** in **Settings › Updates**). If the new version doesn't start properly, the one you had is put back. Your notes are never touched. See [Updates](install-and-update.md#updates).

**Does it work on Mac or Linux?**
Not yet. Dobo is for Windows for now.

**Is Dobo free?**
Yes, and it's free software (GPL-3.0). The source code is on [github.com/sookera/dobo](https://github.com/sookera/dobo).
